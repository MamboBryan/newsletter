## Firebase App Distribution with GitHub Actions

![Firebase App Distribution with GitHub Actions](../assets/Firebase%20App%20Distribution%20with%20GitHub%20Actions.webp)

To kickstart the rather chilly day ❄️, [Blannon Ngoge](https://x.com/blannymichaels) introduced the audience to Firebase App Distribution with GitHub Actions. Get it? "Firebase" and "chilly day"? Anyway, through this practical session, the attendees got to learn how to distribute their Mobile Apps for Testing 🧪, using GitHub Actions, which is probably one of if not the most efficient way of Mobile App Distribution 🔁...

### Motii

<center>

![Motii](../assets/Motii.webp)

</center>

If you have been following us for a while now, as you should, then you know too well that some of us love German Machines 🚗 and stuff. Well, maybe a little too much for one of us in particular 😜. Anyway, we are excited to showcase a product that will not only revolutionise the Car market in Kenya, but will also come in handy when you go buy your very own German Machine or otherwise 😮. Earlier this month, [The Chief Senior Dishwasher, GDE](https://x.com/mambo_bryan) shipped Motii, which is a Mobile App that provides up to date current CRSP for vehicles in Kenya. Why should you get ripped off with this kind of power in your hands? 🤨

Check out Motii on Google Play Store here 👉🏽: [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.bizilabs.app.motii&pcampaignid=web_share](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.bizilabs.app.motii&pcampaignid=web_share)
