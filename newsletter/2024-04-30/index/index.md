## Exploring Canvas in Jetpack Compose for Better UI Experiences 🎨

![Alt text](../assets/Exploring%20Canvas%20in%20Jetpack%20Compose%20for%20Better%20UI%20Experiences.webp)

[The Hutsy Developer](https://twitter.com/mwania_josphat) made good use of an ice breaker (if you know you know) ⛏️ to introduce [his session](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1hJ-Wnu6GoYvWitDqc_ocxP525Uff8zvyut1tUqIjVrQ/edit#slide=id.g2cd78793cc9_0_36) on working with Canvas in Jetpack Compose. Like the Tech Bro that he is, Josphat Mwania used the Audi logo to demonstrate how to work with Canvas in Jetpack Compose using the [Canvas API](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/graphics/Canvas). Josphat concluded his session by challenging the audience to apply what they learnt to draw either the Instagram logo or the Facebook logo. [The Senior Teaman](https://twitter.com/chepsi_) took up the challenge and submitted [this solution](https://gist.github.com/chepsi/2f83b85bbcab08bbc35feba158fd43b2).

## Configuring WorkManager: Running Tasks in The Background 🔧

![Alt text](../assets/Configuring%20WorkManager%20Running%20Tasks%20in%20The%20Background.webp)

Finally, we were served (pun intended but keep reading even if you do not understand) with a taste of Background Process Scheduling using WorkManager by [Joel Muraguri](https://twitter.com/_JoelMuraguri). He used a simple Reminder app to demonstrate the steps needed to implement WorkManager in Android to schedule 🗓️ background tasks.
